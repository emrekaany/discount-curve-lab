# Data and code provenance

This public demo is a clean-room educational implementation of the published
Nelson-Siegel-Svensson family of term-structure models.

- Every row in `data/synthetic_market_data.csv` is fictional and created for this repository.
- The fixture is not a market feed, valuation source, customer extract, employer dataset, or
  historical quote archive.
- The runtime contains no proprietary schema, account, recipient, infrastructure, workstation,
  or organization identifiers.
- No generated curve is suitable for trading, accounting, pricing, reserving, risk, or regulatory
  reporting without independent validation and controlled data sources.
- Discount factors are deterministic mathematical derivatives of the generated synthetic zero
  rates under the documented continuous-compounding convention; they are not vendor quotes.
- The SVG/PNG artwork in `assets/` is original repository artwork generated from fictional curve
  shapes and contains no provider logo, customer screenshot, or proprietary market data.

Repository contributors must not submit real customer, employer, market-vendor, or personal data.
When contributing a new fixture, document its source and redistribution terms.
